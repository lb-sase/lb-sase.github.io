Honoring Asian Excellence.

Welcome to SASE!

Here, we advocate for "the advancement of Asian heritage scientists and engineers in education and employment so that they can achieve their full career potential."

In short, we provide resources to help you develop your professional profile + career, alongside a community of individuals that will support you and your future!

The Three Pillars of SASE

As a nationally recognized organization, these three pillars form the foundation of SASE's values and remind people about the commitment that SASE strives to provide all active members.

Professional

We prepare Asian heritage scientists and engineers for success in the global business world.

Culture

We celebrate diversity on campus and in the workplace.

Community

We provide opportunities for members to make contributions to their local communities.
